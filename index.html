About me

I don't fill vacancies. I find people.

Anyone can post a job and wait. My work starts where job boards stop: the passive candidates who aren't looking, the niche skills only a few hundred people in India have, and the roles that have been open for months.

I've hired across IT, SAP, engineering and non-tech functions for Infosys BPM, Infosys Limited, EdgeVerve and Airbus India, and for clients like Capgemini, TCS and Wipro, from IIT and IIM campus drives to senior niche hires.

Behind every closure is planning: strategic and contingent workforce planning, demand and gap analysis, and pipelines built before the requisition arrives.
